Everything you need to master your finances

A powerful suite of tools designed to help you analyze, track, and forecast your financial health with absolute privacy.

100% Local Processing

We never store or share your bank statements. All data is processed completely offline directly on your device.

Smart Categorization

Our intelligent engine automatically groups your transactions into categories like Food, Utilities, and Shopping.

Visual Analytics

Gain insights instantly with beautiful, interactive charts that track your income and expenses over time.

AI-Powered Forecasts

Predict your future balances and spending trends based on your historical transaction data.

No Bank Login Needed

We will never ask for your bank credentials. Simply upload your standard PDF or Excel statements directly.

Universal Export

Export your consolidated, cleanly categorized data to Excel or CSV format at any time for your personal records.
