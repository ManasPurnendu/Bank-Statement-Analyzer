How It Works

Analyze your statements in 4 simple steps

1. Upload Statement

Drag and drop your PDF or Excel statement. Encrypted statements are supported.

2. Local Decryption

Password-protected files are decrypted in-memory locally. No data leaves your machine.

3. Auto-Categorization

Transactions are auto-tagged by category. Create custom rules to classify future items.

4. Analytics & Forecast

Explore spending trends, subscription protection, future cash flows, and generate PDFs.
